# Experiment 6 - Image Recognition

**Run:** Open `project.json` and click **Run**. It opens the included local `Data/image_target_demo.html` page in your default browser.

**Native UiPath activity chain for your practical:** `Use Application/Browser` -> `Find Image` (indicate the blue **TARGET - CLICK ME** button) -> use an If/verification step -> `Click Image` -> `Message Box` to confirm success. Also test the not-found branch.

**Important:** Image targets must be captured on your computer. Their match can change with screen resolution, display scaling, theme, or page layout. The included launcher opens a stable practice target but does not pre-store a machine-specific screenshot. Pages 18-20 of your record show Find Image, Click Image and the success/not-found outputs.
